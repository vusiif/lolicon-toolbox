zh_wordlm.bin - Chinese word frequency model for lossy GBK recovery

Derived from the jieba main dictionary (jieba/dict.txt) by Sun Junyi,
https://github.com/fxsjy/jieba, retrieved on 2026-11-10 from
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fxsjy/jieba/master/jieba/dict.txt
(SHA256 7197C3211DDD98962B036CDF40324D1EA2BFAA12BD028E68FAA70111A88E12A8).
jieba is released under the MIT License; see jieba-LICENSE.txt.

The file is a prefix tree of the dictionary words made of GBK encodable
Chinese characters whose frequency is at least 5, with each word's jieba
frequency. Regenerate it with:

    python -I scripts/build_zh_wordlm.py path/to/jieba/dict.txt 5
